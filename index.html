drew@drewconway.com  ·  New York, NY  ·  LinkedIn  ·  drewconway.com

Drew Conway has spent two decades operating at the intersection of artificial intelligence, private capital, and enterprise transformation. He founded Two Sigma Real Estate, the firm's institutional real estate investment business. As founder of Alluvium, he built an industrial-AI software company and exited it into a now-unicorn acquirer. With DataKind, he co-founded the global nonprofit defining responsible data-science practice. His career began inside the U.S. intelligence community, applying graph analysis to counterterrorism problems before "data science" had a name. Across every chapter, the same operating thesis: technical capability is the input; commercial outcome is the test.

Alluvium

Founder & Chief Executive Officer  ·  Acquired by Augury

Drew founded Alluvium, an industrial-AI software company applying real-time and forensic machine learning to manufacturing and energy operations — markets where downtime is measured in millions per hour.

  • Established early strategic partnerships with ExxonMobil and Honeywell, applying proprietary ML to operational decision systems on critical industrial infrastructure.
  • Acquired by Augury in January 2019 (acquisition terms undisclosed), concurrent with Augury's $25M Series C led by Insight Partners.
  • Augury has since reached unicorn status as the category leader in machine-health AI.
  • Integrated Alluvium's platform and team into Augury's U.S. operations through transition.

2011 – Present · Global

DataKind

Co-Founder & Board Member

Drew co-founded DataKind, the global nonprofit connecting practicing data scientists with mission-driven organizations — built before the data-science profession had a name, and now the canonical institution for pro-bono and ethically-grounded data work.

  • 20,000+ volunteer practitioner network; partnerships across global development, public health, and human-rights organizations.
2013 – 2014 · New York, NY

NYC Mayor's Office of Data Analytics

Senior Advisor

Drew served as direct advisor to NYC's first Chief Data Officer, applying analytics to operational outcomes across FDNY, the Department of Buildings, Finance, and Sanitation — the early playbook for how a large municipality can use its own data to improve service delivery.

2011 – 2013 · New York, NY

IA Ventures

Data Scientist-in-Residence

Drew was among the first in-house data scientists embedded in a venture firm. He partnered with portfolio companies on technical strategy, product, and customer growth, and conducted technical due diligence on early-stage investments informing seed capital allocation.

2005 – 2008 · Washington, DC & Forward Deployments

U.S. Intelligence Community

All-Source Analyst (via Booz Allen Hamilton)

Drew began his career applying graph analysis and early machine-learning techniques to counterterrorism intelligence problems for U.S. defense agencies. He provided direct analytical support to forward-deployed operations in Iraq and Afghanistan — among the first practitioners to bring those methods into all-source intelligence workflows.
