Spring Beak Hotel Options

Highlights

  • Dorado Beach really is the unicorn here: Bonvoy and on both premium card platforms. If you liked the polish + space + privacy + service at Domes, it is the closest Caribbean analog — just Caribbean-tropical instead of Greek-Mediterranean. ￼
  • Four Seasons Anguilla / Cap Juluca / Eden Roc are the “Domes-y but not on Bonvoy” set — so your leverage is Amex Platinum FHR (and sometimes Chase Edit) rather than hotel points. ￼
  • Hyatt Ziva Cap Cana sneaks in because it’s on Amex The Hotel Collection for Platinum, so you can still get a credit + breakfast; it also checks the “we have tweens and need stuff to do” box. ￼
  • Shore Club needs an eyes-on check inside your Amex or Chase login when you actually book — I can’t see the specific line item because Amex blocks scraping — so I’ve left it as “verify.” ￼

Loyalty Codes

  • MB = Marriott Bonvoy earn/redeem (or Ritz-Carlton Reserve now participating). ￼
  • FHR = Amex Platinum – Fine Hotels & Resorts (guaranteed breakfast for 2, 12/4pm benefits, property credit) — property shows on Amex FHR right now. ￼
  • THC = Amex Platinum – The Hotel Collection (still useful, especially for all-inclusive / family resorts) — e.g. Hyatt Ziva Cap Cana. ￼
  • CSR = Chase Sapphire Reserve – The Edit / LHRC (breakfast + $100-ish credit + upgrade).
